Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short: ThaiWords collects no personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not use the internet.

About this policy

ThaiWords is a Thai spelling game for Android phones. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

None. ThaiWords does not collect, send, share or sell any personal information. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

Information kept on your phone

So your progress is still there next time, the app saves the following on your phone only:

  • your score: words solved, first-try answers and current streak
  • your chosen mode, category and font

This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Speech

Words and sentences are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech engine. ThaiWords only passes it the Thai text shown on screen. If the app offers to install a Thai voice, it opens your phone's voice settings; any download there is handled by your phone's speech service under that service's own privacy policy.

Children

ThaiWords does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
